# Privacy / 隐私
Profiles stay in user-controlled local directories outside public source. Private Git versioning is optional; no upload/sync exists. Only confirmed, shareable, non-sensitive records enter reads/exports; pending/private remain excluded. Preview, human semantic review, exact hash approval and recipient review precede sharing.

Regex detects common secret/contact/numeric-ID/filename patterns, but misses meanings, split values and obfuscations. Findings show category/location, not matched values. A clean scan does not establish absence of privacy risks or grant sharing permission. Exact reviewed synthetic-source exceptions are line-hash-bound; whole-file bypasses are forbidden.

Data/backups are unencrypted. OS permissions and optional disk encryption are user-managed. A hostile same-user writer can bypass consent artifacts; portable path checks cannot fully eliminate substitution races. Historical snapshots do not refresh automatically. Removing a current fact does not erase every backup.

私人档案与备份不得提交本项目。只使用明确可分享的最少资料；隐私正则不能替代人工逐项审查，不能把 CLEAN 当作授权。跨平台共享需确认接收者权限和用途。本包仅有虚构资料，不应加入真实家庭、客户、财务或认证内容。
